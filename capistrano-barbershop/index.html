About the shop

The way it used to be.

Capistrano Barbershop is owned and operated by Faraj Capistrano, a Master Barber providing old world flavor and service of days gone by.

We provide traditional and short haircuts as well as contemporary men's hairstyles, in a classy, nostalgic setting full of interesting men and great conversation.

Gentlemen, come in and lose yourself in a genuinely old-fashioned barber shop experience.
